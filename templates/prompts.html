2. Tailor a CV Block

Reword an existing CV bullet to mirror the JD's language.

You are a CV writing expert for senior Linux/infrastructure engineers targeting Germany and EU.

CV block to tailor:
[PASTE YOUR CV BLOCK HERE]

Target job description (or key phrases from it):
[PASTE JD EXCERPT OR KEY PHRASES]

Rules:
- Keep the factual content identical — do not invent metrics or tools
- Mirror the JD's terminology and keyword choices
- Write in third-person, past tense, active verbs (managed, automated, deployed…)
- Maximum 3 concise bullet points
- Do NOT add fluff like "contributed to" or "helped with"
- If the JD is for a German company, output a German version below the English one

Location: [LOCATION]

Max 250 words.

5. Interview Prep (Linux/HPC/DevOps)

Generate likely technical questions based on a JD.

You are a senior Linux/HPC interviewer. Generate a realistic technical interview question set for this role.

Job description / key tech stack: [PASTE JD OR KEY SKILLS]

Produce:
1. 5 Linux sysadmin deep-dives (networking, kernel, filesystems, troubleshooting, performance)
2. 3 Ansible / automation questions
3. 3 HPC-specific questions if applicable (Slurm, InfiniBand, PXE, GPU)
4. 2 CI/CD / DevOps questions (Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes if in JD)
5. 2 behavioral questions typical for German companies (Arbeitsweise, Konflikte)
6. For each question: provide a strong answer outline (not a full answer — just the key points to hit)

Calibrate difficulty to "senior level, 10+ years Linux experience."
6. Salary Research (Germany)

Understand what to ask for before negotiating.

I am researching salary ranges in Germany for a senior Linux/HPC Administrator role. Help me think through this.

Role details:
- Title: [TITLE FROM JD]
- City: [CITY]
- Company type: [research institute / mid-size tech / enterprise / startup]
- Years of experience: 10+
- Key skills: Linux, Ansible, HPC/Slurm, Terraform, Docker, Python

Please provide:
1. Gross salary range (Brutto) realistic for 2025 Germany for this profile and city
2. How Tarifvertrag (collective bargaining) affects research institute roles (TVöD, TV-L)
3. What benefits to negotiate beyond salary (Homeoffice-Tage, Weiterbildungsbudget, Jobticket)
4. How to phrase salary expectations when asked "Was sind Ihre Gehaltsvorstellungen?"
5. Red lines — what's too low to accept for this profile
7. Cold Outreach (LinkedIn / XING)

Message a hiring manager or team lead directly.

Write a short cold outreach message (LinkedIn or XING) from Richard Eseke to a hiring manager or team lead at [COMPANY].

Context:
- Richard is a senior Linux/HPC Administrator looking for roles in Germany/EU
- He saw the [TITLE] posting and wants to reach out directly, not just apply through the ATS
- He has a specific connection point: [ONE THING ABOUT THE COMPANY/TEAM that genuinely interests him]

Message rules:
- Max 4 sentences
- Do NOT open with "I hope this message finds you well"
- Reference something specific about their tech stack or work
- End with a low-friction ask (15-min call, happy to share CV)
- Professional but human — not a form letter
- Offer an English AND a German version
8. Follow-up After Applying

After 10-14 days with no response.

Write a brief follow-up email from Richard Eseke to [COMPANY] regarding his application for [TITLE], submitted [DATE].

Tone: professional, not desperate, respectful of their timeline.
Length: 3-4 sentences max.
Goal: confirm receipt, reiterate interest, invite them to reach out with questions.
Do not be passive-aggressive about the wait time.

Provide:
1. English version
2. German version (formal Sie form)
